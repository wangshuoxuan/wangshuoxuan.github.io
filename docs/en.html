Research

Areas of interest
  1. 01

    Memristive devices

    Programmable conductance, device nonidealities, and the relationship between device characteristics and circuit behavior.

  2. 02

    Compute-in-memory

    The interplay of memory and computation, including trade-offs in precision, data conversion, data movement, and system energy.

  3. 03

    Neuromorphic computing

    Connections between devices, circuits, and computational models, and how computation can be implemented under hardware constraints.

These are research interests. Specific projects, experimental results, and publications will be added when confirmed.
