Say hello. Let’s get to know each other.

I’m glad you’re here, and you’re welcome to say hello. Whatever your age, field, work, or life experience, I’d be happy to hear from you. We don’t need similar backgrounds or fully formed opinions. I’m especially curious about perspectives and experiences different from my own.

I set aside time each week to meet new people and deepen existing friendships. We can discuss an idea, learn or make something together, or simply have coffee, take a walk, and try something new.

If you have a question, could use some help, or just want to get acquainted, please reach out. You don’t need to arrive with something to offer—curiosity, sincerity, and mutual respect are a good start.

A simple way to start

Not sure how to begin? A few lines are enough: what you’d like me to call you, something you’re interested in, and what you’d enjoy discussing or doing together. On WeChat, mentioning that you found me through this website helps me recognize you.

For example: “Hi, I found your website and have been getting interested in urban history. Would you be up for a walk or coffee to exchange observations?”

There’s no need to be formal or answer every prompt. For a meetup, your city and a few possible times help. If I haven’t replied, a gentle follow-up after a few days is welcome. I look forward to getting to know you.
